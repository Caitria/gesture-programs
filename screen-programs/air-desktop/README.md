# Air Desktop

A desk of virtual cards on the camera view — and, since v4, my
decision desk. Ordinary cards (a photo card reading a folder of
pictures, notes cards backed by a real text file, a status card) sit
alongside one gold **decision card** at a time.

Decisions come from a plain-text queue file; answers are appended to
an answers file. On my setup both files live in a synced cloud
folder, so my assistant stocks the queue and reads the verdicts —
the desk is a two-way channel, not a toy.

- **Move cards** by pinching them; resize with a two-hand stretch.
  Cards stay exactly where you put them.
- **Answer a decision** by holding its card in a lit zone — YES
  (right), NO (left), DEFER (top) — for a short beat, or release it
  there, or press Y / X / D. Holding it on the TRASH pad also counts
  as DEFER.
- **Delete a card** by holding it on the TRASH pad for half a
  second. A card merely resting there is safe. U undoes a deletion.
- **Dictate** with thumbs-up (start) and thumbs-down (stop). Tap a
  notes card first to arm it (a red dot shows in its title bar) and
  the words land in the notes file and appear on the card; dictate
  unarmed and the note goes to the answers file instead, aimed at
  the current decision. Transcription is on-device (macOS Speech).
- **Capture** a new card from the camera by holding a two-hand frame
  steady for about a second.
